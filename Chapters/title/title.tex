\begin{abstract}
The 2022-24 NA62 data has been used to search for the dark photon ($A'$) from the $\pi^0 \to A'\gamma$ and the subsequent prompt decay $A' \to e^+ e^-$. Within the mass range s $6 \leq m_{A'} \leq 121.6$\,\si{\mega\electronvolt\per\clight\squared} no signal was observed and 
an exclusion of the mixing parameter $\epsilon^2$ across the mass range is obtained.

The NA62 beam consists of only 6\,\% kaons which require identification which is conducted by the KTAG. The KTAG consists of a \gls{CEDAR} and a purpose built photon-detection system. Since 2016 NA62 has employed a \gls{CEDAR} with a \gls{n2} radiator gas.
To reduce the amount of material in the path of the beam a new \gls{CEDAR} utilising \gls{h2} as a radiator gas can be used. Its design, testing and commissioning at NA62 is presented.
\end{abstract}
