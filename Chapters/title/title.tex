\begin{abstract}
The 2022-24 NA62 data has been used to search for the dark photon ($A'$) from the $\pi^0 \to A'\gamma$ and the subsequent prompt decay $A' \to e^+ e^-$. Within the mass range $6 \leq m_{A'} \leq 121.6$\,\si{\mega\electronvolt\per\clight\squared} no signal was observed and 
an exclusion limit on the mixing parameter $\epsilon^2$ across the mass range is obtained.

The NA62 beam consists of only 6\,\% kaons, therefore requiring beam particle identification conducted by the KTAG. The KTAG comprises a \gls{CEDAR} and a purpose-built photon-detection system. Since 2016, NA62 has employed a \gls{CEDAR} with a \gls{n2} radiator gas.
To reduce the amount of material in the beam's path, a new \gls{CEDAR} utilising \gls{h2} as a radiator gas has been used since 2024. Its design, testing and commissioning at NA62 are presented. The \gls{CEDAR} with \gls{h2} produced a kaon identification efficiency of 99.7\,\% and a kaon time resolution of 66\,ps.

Finally, software developments towards the design of a new High Intensity Kaon Experiments (HIKE) facility are presented.
\end{abstract}
